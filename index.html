    What the rules add
    1. Gates at 25%, 50% and 70% of the window, plus a Day 2 check
    2. One owner and one blocker for every migration
    3. Automatic follow-up when no one has touched an account for 3 business days
    4. Estimated delay by checkpoint owner; verify the cause with the studio
    5. A proactive update that names the source platform and its usual export wait
    6. Three delivery lanes, with a commit date that runs lane work and the payment transfer in parallel and checks it against the two-week target
    7. A zero-tolerance cutover audit: five record counts must reconcile and five pre-launch checks must pass
